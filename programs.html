Programs

Maple Grove offers French immersion from kindergarten to grade 12 at two dual-track schools, with bus service available from every catchment area in the division.

Our outdoor education program takes grade 7 students on a three-day camp each spring, and the high school skilled-trades centre offers carpentry, welding and automotive courses that count toward apprenticeship hours.

Early learning programs for four-year-olds run in the mornings at Birchwood and Cedar Hill schools. Registration opens every February.
