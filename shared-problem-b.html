Bus Routes and Transportation

Maple Grove School Division operates 38 bus routes covering rural and town catchment areas. Students who live more than 1.6 km from their school are eligible for transportation.

Route changes are posted each August. Families will be contacted by phone if a bus is delayed by more than 20 minutes because of weather or road conditions.

Students must be at their stop five minutes before the scheduled pickup time. All routes on this page are fictional.
