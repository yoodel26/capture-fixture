Regional Science Fair Draws Record Entries

Published October 21, 2026

A record 148 projects were judged at this year's Maple Grove regional science fair, held in the Riverside Collegiate gymnasium. Projects ranged from soil erosion models to a solar-powered bird feeder.

Grade 8 student Avery Placeholder won the top junior award for a study of how road salt affects local pond water. Twelve finalists will represent the division at the provincial fair next spring.
