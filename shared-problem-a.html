Kindergarten Registration

Kindergarten registration for the 2027–28 school year opens on February 1. Children who turn five by December 31, 2027 are eligible to enrol.

Families should register at their catchment school and bring proof of age and address. French immersion kindergarten has limited space and is filled in order of registration.

Kindergarten orientation evenings are held at every elementary school in May. All dates on this page are synthetic.
