About the Division

Maple Grove School Division was formed in 1962 when four rural school districts agreed to share buses, teachers and a single board office. Today the division is governed by a board of seven elected trustees who meet on the second Tuesday of each month.

Our mission is to help every learner grow in confidence, curiosity and care for others. The division's three-year plan focuses on early reading, mathematics achievement and student wellbeing.

Board meeting minutes and budget summaries are now published monthly, and annual reports each June (updated in fixture version 2). All figures on this page are fictional and exist only for testing.
